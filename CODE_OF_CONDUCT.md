# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, no harassment, no spam or self-promotion without disclosure. Discussions here happen in English, Arabic and Persian — all are welcome.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **nexorah.ai@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers may remove content or block accounts that break these rules.
